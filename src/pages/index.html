What is 8K Strong IPTV?+

An internet-based television service providing live channels, movies, series and sports through compatible players and devices.

Which devices are supported?+

Smart TVs, Android TV, Fire TV, Apple devices, phones, tablets, computers, MAG boxes and compatible IPTV applications.

How quickly is access activated?+

Access details are generally prepared shortly after payment and delivered through your selected contact method.

What connection speed is recommended?+

Around 10 Mbps can be suitable for HD, while 4K usually benefits from 25 Mbps or more and a stable local connection.

Can I become a reseller?+

Yes. Reseller packages provide credits and panel access for creating and managing customer subscriptions.
